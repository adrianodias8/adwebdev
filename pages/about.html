About me

I'm a developer from Portugal, living in Brussels. I like building new products and learning new technology.

Short bio

My core expertise is PHP, especially Drupal and Laravel, and I'm just as interested in Linux, Docker and newer things like LLMs. Honestly, I like all things tech. Away from the code you'll usually find me taking photographs, or spending time with my wife, who helps me with design and with life in general.

Career

I started in web development at 17, with an internship through my vocational high school, just as Drupal 7 was released. That sparked my interest in building for the web, and I've been at it ever since. For the past five years I've worked as a contractor at the European Commission, delivering web solutions that scale.

Lately: AI

I've been playing with AI a lot, and alongside my Drupal and Laravel work I now also offer consultancy.
